# Three-design HeroCarousel

1. Copy `HeroCarousel.jsx` into your components folder.
2. Copy `HeroCarousel.css` beside it.
3. Copy the three PNG files into `src/assets/`.
4. If your component lives somewhere else, adjust the `../api/api` and `../assets/...` imports.

The carousel automatically advances every 6.5 seconds, pauses during hover/focus, supports arrow keys and reduced-motion preferences, and keeps the university strip connected to `/public/clients`.

Required packages already used by your original component: `react`, `react-router-dom`, and `lucide-react`.
