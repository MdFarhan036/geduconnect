About Vivekananda Global University Online

Established in 2012 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, Vivekananda Global University (VGU) is a multidisciplinary private university set up by the Rajasthan State Legislature under the Vivekananda Global University, Jaipur Act (Act No. 11/2012) . Founded by the Bagru Raje Educational Society (BRES), VGU draws inspiration from Swami Vivekananda's vision of education as a force for personal and societal transformation.

Recognising the growing demand for accessible, career-oriented higher education, VGU launched its dedicated Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) . VGU Online delivers digitally enabled undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs for working professionals, entrepreneurs, defence personnel and fresh graduates.
